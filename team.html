Learn together.
Compete together.

Science Olympiad turns biology, chemistry, earth science, engineering, and inquiry into a team competition.

Prepare with a partner.

Members turn event rules into notes, practice tests, lab routines, and working devices. Most competitors balance three or four events with one or two partners in each.

Grow into leadership.

Experienced competitors become event captains, organize resources, and guide new members through the season.

How the team runs.

Students lead. Coaches create continuity.

Executive board
Coordinates the season, communication, tournaments, and club culture.
Event captains
Organize resources, run practice, and help partners make progress.
Coaches
Provide technical guidance, safety oversight, and competition-day support.

Common questions.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Captains and coaches help new members learn from the ground up.

How much time does it take?

Expect a weekly meeting plus independent study or build time. Preparation increases near tournaments.

What is the difference between build and study events?

Build events involve designing a device. Study and lab events focus on knowledge, analysis, and hands-on procedures. Many members do both.

When can I join?

Recruiting is strongest at the start of the school year, but you can contact us any time.

Come meet the team.

Weekly meetings happen after school in room A101.
